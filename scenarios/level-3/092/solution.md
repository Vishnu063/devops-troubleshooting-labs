# Solution · 092

## Diagnosis supported by supplied evidence

Upgrade surge needs additional capacity that quota does not permit.

## Investigate

Inspect upgrade events, VM quota, subnet capacity, surge settings, and disruption budgets.

Correlate timestamps and recent changes. A similar symptom in a real environment can have a different cause.

## Recovery

Secure approved capacity or use a supported maintenance strategy preserving availability.

## Verify

Upgrade progresses and workloads remain healthy.

## Prevent recurrence

Preflight quota, addresses, and disruption constraints before upgrades.

## Interview follow-ups

- What evidence would invalidate this diagnosis?
- What availability or data risk does the repair introduce?
- How would you roll back if verification fails?
- What would you tell the incident commander in 60 seconds?
