# Solution · 070

## Diagnosis supported by supplied evidence

The rollout permits all serving capacity to disappear.

## Investigate

Inspect rollout strategy, readiness, replica availability, and termination timing.

Correlate timestamps and recent changes. A similar symptom in a real environment can have a different cause.

## Recovery

Choose availability-preserving surge and unavailable settings with sufficient capacity.

## Verify

Continuous traffic succeeds through a fresh rollout.

## Prevent recurrence

Test rollout behavior under traffic and capacity constraints.

## Interview follow-ups

- What evidence would invalidate this diagnosis?
- What availability or data risk does the repair introduce?
- How would you roll back if verification fails?
- What would you tell the incident commander in 60 seconds?
