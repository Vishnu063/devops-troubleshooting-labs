# Solution · 044

## Diagnosis supported by supplied evidence

No available runner satisfies all job tags.

## Investigate

Inspect job tags, runner tags, protection, and runner status.

Correlate timestamps and recent changes. A similar symptom in a real environment can have a different cause.

## Recovery

Assign the correct approved runner or correct the job tags.

## Verify

The job executes on the intended isolated runner.

## Prevent recurrence

Document runner capabilities and protected workload routing.
